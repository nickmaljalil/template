Help

Quick answers first. If you still need us, send a message.

How do I place an order?

Open the Menu page, tap Add on any dish, and check your total at the top. Pay at the counter when you pick up.

Do you have vegan or gluten-free food?

Yes. Items marked Vegan have no animal products. Ask us about gluten-free bread for any wrap.

What are your hours?

Monday to Thursday 11am–9pm, Friday and Saturday 11am–11pm, Sunday 12pm–8pm.

Something was wrong with my order

Tell us right away using the form. We will remake the dish or refund you.

Can I book for a group?

Groups of 8 or more can reserve a long table. Send us the date and headcount.
